## 7. gaiia analysis

**Verified facts:** A US application **GAIIA (Serial 97262346)** exists, filed **2022-02-10**, covering **Classes 9, 38, 42**, owned by a corporation. The brand `gaiia` (gaiia.com) is, from open-web evidence, a funded OSS/BSS platform for ISPs — i.e., **the same product category as GAAhex.**

**Likelihood-of-confusion assessment (DuPont-type factors, preliminary):**
- **Relatedness of goods/services — VERY HIGH.** Identical real-world field; **identical class coverage (9/38/42).** This is the dominant factor and it points toward confusion.
- **Sound — MODERATE overlap.** Both lead with "GA(A)-"; tails differ ("-iia" vs "-hex").
- **Sight — MODERATE difference.** GAIIA vs GAAHEX share the first three letters; overall silhouettes differ.
- **Meaning — DIFFERENT.** "Gaia/earth" vs "hexagon/hex."
- **Channels/customers — LIKELY SHARED** (ISPs).

**Conclusion:** A genuine likelihood-of-confusion concern in software/SaaS/OSS-BSS/telecom. **🟠 Orange now**, because GAIIA's current status is unverified and the marks are not identical. **🔴 Red** if counsel confirms GAIIA 97262346 (or a successor) is **registered and live** in 9/38/42 — in which case it could be cited against a GAAhex application, or gaiia could oppose.
