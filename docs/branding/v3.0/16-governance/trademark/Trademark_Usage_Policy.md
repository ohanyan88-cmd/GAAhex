# GAAhex™ — Trademark Usage Policy

**Approved notation:** **GAAhex™**
**Version:** v2.0 · **Status:** active

## 1. Current approved notation
The approved trademark notation is **GAAhex™**. The ™ symbol indicates a claimed trademark and **may be used immediately, without registration.**

## 2. ™ vs ®
- **™** — use now. It asserts common-law/unregistered trademark rights and requires no registration.
- **®** — **must NOT be used** until a registration is formally granted by the relevant trademark office. Using ® before registration is improper and, in some jurisdictions, unlawful. Switch ™ → ® only after a registration certificate issues, and only in the jurisdiction(s) where the registration is granted.

## 3. Preferred public usage
- **First mention** in a document, page, or section: **GAAhex™**
- **Subsequent mentions:** **GAAhex** (no symbol)
- Do not place ™ on every occurrence. One marked first mention per document/page is the professional standard.
- In running prose, treat **GAAhex** as a proper adjective/name; avoid pluralizing or possessive-izing the mark where practical.

## 4. Where ™ is applied
Apply **GAAhex™** (first mention) in:
- Brand documentation, specifications, operations manual, roadmaps
- README files, release notes, changelogs
- Governance and trademark documentation
- Developer documentation and usage examples
- Marketing templates, presentation templates
- Email-signature templates (in template text/comments, not on the logo image)

## 5. Where ™ is NOT applied
The visual logo and all artwork remain **GAAhex** with **no ™**:
- Logo artwork (SVG/PNG/JPG lockups), icon-only marks, logomarks, monograms, wordmarks
- Favicons, PWA icons, social avatars, watermarks
- Source masters (.ai, .svg, .eps, .pdf) and export renders
- App/OS name labels rendered from icon/manifest assets

Rationale: the ™ symbol is a textual legal notice, not part of the mark's design. Embedding it in artwork would alter the locked logo geometry and is not permitted.

## 6. Legal & marketing materials
Marketing and legal materials **may** display **GAAhex™** (first mention) as textual notice. This does not change the logo. If/when registration is granted, update textual notices to **GAAhex®** in the granted jurisdiction(s) and record the registration details in the trademark governance folder.

## 7. Notes
This policy is an internal usage standard, not legal advice. Formal trademark clearance and registration must be handled by a qualified trademark attorney. See the clearance research in this `trademark/` folder where present.
