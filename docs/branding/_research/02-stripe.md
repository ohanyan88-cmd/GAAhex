# Stripe — brand reference notes

Source: `stripe.com/newsroom/brand-assets` (WebFetch, 2026-06-04)

## Why Stripe is a peer reference for GAAhex

Stripe is the canonical example of a fintech/infra brand that earned trust at
operator scale. GAAhex's M1-C work stream literally integrates Stripe (per
`portal-m1-strategy.md`), so the brand will live side-by-side with Stripe's in
the product (Stripe Elements rendered inside GAAhex Pay views). The brand has
to not embarrass itself next to that surface.

## Logo system — three colors only

- *"The Stripe logo is available in three colors."*
- Slate and "blurple" for light backgrounds.
- White for dark backgrounds.
- Explicit rule: *"Do not use any other color for the wordmark."*

Lesson for GAAhex: limit logo color variants to the same three:
- **Cobalt+Gold** (full color, dark background — current primary)
- **Mono cobalt** (light background, no gold)
- **Mono platinum / white** (dark background, monochrome — quiet contexts)

Anything else is rejected.

## Legal scaffolding

Brand usage governed by Stripe's "Marks Usage Agreement." Every brand asset
download is under those terms.

Lesson for GAAhex: doesn't apply at M1 (no third-party usage). But mark a
future placeholder in the brand bible — once partners start linking to GAAhex,
a one-page MARKS_USAGE.md becomes necessary.

## What Stripe's brand page intentionally doesn't include

The public newsroom page omits clear space rules, typography standards, voice
principles, and do/don'ts. Stripe keeps those internal. Lesson: GAAhex's brand
bible should be **more open than Stripe's** — we're working in Armenian +
English, partner-driven from day 1, and the openness *is* the brand.
