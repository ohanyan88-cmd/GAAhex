# Linear — brand reference notes

Source: `linear.app/brand` (WebFetch, 2026-06-04)

## Why Linear is a primary anchor for GAAhex

Gev explicitly named Linear as a taste reference (alongside Notion and Tailscale)
during the brand kickoff. Linear is the cleanest public B2B SaaS brand system in
the "operator-focused, flat-vector, anti-luxury" register that GAAhex is aiming
for. Most of what Linear does in restraint, GAAhex should mirror.

## Logo system (three components — hierarchical)

1. **Wordmark** — primary asset. Linear's exact line: *"The Linear wordmark
   should be used in all references to Linear as space allows. Monochrome usage
   is preferred with the brand colors below."*
2. **Logomark** — concise alternative for constrained layouts, but the
   *"wordmark has stronger brand recognition."*
3. **Icon** — stylized mark, acceptable for social media and chips with *"an
   appropriate corner radius."*

Lesson for GAAhex: the wordmark should be the default. The standalone mark
(`GAAhex-mark.svg`) is for favicons, app icons, social avatars — not the default
brand surface.

## Naming convention (strict)

- Always capitalized as a single word: **Linear**, never "Linear app."
- Specific product releases are treated as proper nouns (e.g., *"Linear Method"*).

Lesson for GAAhex: lock the capitalization. It's **GAAhex** (all five letters as
one camel-mixed word, the "GAA" carrying the family weight). Never "gaahex,"
never "Gaahex," never "GAAHex." Never "GAAhex app" — just "GAAhex."

## Color philosophy — restraint

- Primary: *"subtle desaturated blue"* — works across light and dark backgrounds.
- Supporting colors: Mercury White (#F4F5F8) and Nordic Gray (#222326) preferred
  for monochrome usage.
- **Brand color is typically reserved for backgrounds.**

Lesson for GAAhex: Linear's "brand color reserved for backgrounds" maps almost
1:1 to D18's "Cobalt = brand spine, structural chrome only." This is the same
discipline GAAhex already locked. Linear is the public confirmation that this
register works at scale.

## Design principle — generous spacing

*"Provide plenty of space around Linear assets... give them room to breathe.
They shouldn't feel cramped or cluttered."*

This is the **clear-space rule** every brand book has. GAAhex needs one too —
expressed in multiples of a letterform (typically the "G" cap-height).

## Brand do/don'ts (Linear's list)

- Don't alter files.
- Don't imply endorsement relationships.
- Don't use assets in product naming.
- Don't combine graphics without written consent.

Lesson for GAAhex: these four are the irreducible misuse rules. Adopt verbatim
form for the GAAhex brand bible.

## What Linear doesn't publish (relevant gap)

Linear's public brand page is intentionally thin on voice/tone — most of the
voice work lives inside the product copy itself. Lesson: GAAhex should publish
a voice doc anyway, because it has more honesty/family register than Linear
does, and we want that codified rather than implicit.
